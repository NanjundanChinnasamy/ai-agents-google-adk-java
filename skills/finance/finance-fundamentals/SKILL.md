# Finance Fundamentals Skill

## Purpose & Scope
This skill provides a concise, structured framework for defining and explaining core equity and financial terms. It ensures explanations are clear, educational, and grounded in standard corporate finance definitions without presenting speculative conclusions.

## Core Concepts & Definitions

### 1. Ownership & Equity
- **Stock / Share**: A unit of equity ownership in a corporation. Shareholders have a residual claim on the company's assets and net earnings after debt obligations are satisfied.
- **Market Capitalization (Market Cap)**: Total market value of a company's outstanding shares.
  $$\text{Market Cap} = \text{Current Share Price} \times \text{Total Shares Outstanding}$$
  - *Large-Cap*: Typically > $10B / large enterprise stability.
  - *Mid-Cap*: Typically $2B–$10B / growth with moderate volatility.
  - *Small-Cap*: Typically < $2B / higher growth potential, higher business risk.

### 2. Operating & Earnings Metrics
- **Revenue (Top Line)**: Total money brought in from sales of goods or services before any costs or expenses are deducted.
- **Net Income / Profit (Bottom Line)**: Residual earnings after all operating expenses, interest, taxes, and depreciation are subtracted from revenue.
- **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**: Net profit apportioned to each individual share of common stock.
  $$\text{EPS} = \frac{\text{Net Income} - \text{Preferred Dividends}}{\text{Weighted Average Shares Outstanding}}$$

### 3. Shareholder Distributions
- **Dividend**: A discretionary distribution of a portion of company earnings paid out to shareholders, typically in cash.
- **Dividend Yield**: Annual dividend payment expressed as a percentage of the current share price.
  $$\text{Dividend Yield} = \frac{\text{Annual Dividend Per Share}}{\text{Current Share Price}} \times 100$$
  *Context*: High dividend yield can reflect shareholder-return focus or a declining share price; it must be assessed against dividend payout sustainability.

### 4. Valuation Multiples
- **Price-to-Earnings (P/E)**: Ratio comparing market price per share to annual earnings per share.
- **Price-to-Book (P/B)**: Ratio comparing market price per share to accounting book value per share (net assets).

### 5. Debt & Liquidity
- **Total Debt**: Sum of short-term borrowings, commercial paper, and long-term notes/bonds owed by the company.
- **Operating Cash Flow (OCF)**: Cash generated strictly from ongoing core business operations.
- **Free Cash Flow (FCF)**: Operating cash flow minus capital expenditures (CapEx). FCF represents cash available for debt repayment, reinvestment, dividends, or acquisitions.

## Guidelines for Responses
1. State the standard corporate definition first.
2. Provide practical context (e.g. how investors interpret the metric).
3. Explicitly state limitations (e.g. accounting variances, non-cash charges).
4. Never state a metric alone guarantees investment performance.
