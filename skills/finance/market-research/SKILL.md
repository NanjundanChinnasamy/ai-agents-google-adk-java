# Market Research Skill

## Purpose & Scope
This skill provides a standardized 8-step methodology to structure and synthesize current equity research. It functions in tandem with real-time web retrieval tools (such as Google Search) to transform disparate news snippets into an institutional, evidence-grounded synthesis.

## The Eight-Stage Research Investigation Structure

When investigating a company, ticker, or current market event, structure findings across these eight sequential dimensions:

### 1. Official Company Announcements
- Corporate governance shifts (C-suite transitions, board changes).
- Capital allocation decisions: Share buybacks, dividend declarations, stock splits, or debt issuance.
- Strategic initiatives: Major M&A, divestitures, joint ventures, or patent filings.

### 2. Recent Financial Results & Filings
- Latest quarterly/annual revenue, EBITDA, and net earnings vs consensus expectations.
- Forward management guidance, capital expenditure (CapEx) outlook, and segment performance.
- Free cash flow generation and balance sheet liquidity updates.

### 3. Regulatory & Legal Developments
- Compliance disclosures, antitrust inquiries, environmental audits, or pending litigation.
- Changes in statutory taxation, tariffs, export controls, or sector-specific licensing.

### 4. Industry & Competitive Developments
- Peer group earnings releases and competitor market share gains/losses.
- Supply chain dynamics, component shortages, freight costs, or vendor pricing pressures.

### 5. Macroeconomic & Factor Influences
- Prevailing interest rate environment, inflation metrics (CPI/PCE), and currency exchange rates.
- Broad equity risk sentiment, liquidity conditions, and sovereign bond yield curve moves.

### 6. Analyst Commentary & Consensus Views
- Recent Wall Street / institutional rating revisions (Upgrades, Downgrades, Initiations).
- Attributed price target updates and consensus earnings revisions with rationale.

### 7. Market Reaction & Price Performance
- Short-term vs 52-week trading trends, volume spikes, and relative performance vs benchmark index.
- Institutional foreign/domestic flows and options/volatility pricing where reported.

### 8. Key Risks & Uncertainties
- Critical catalysts or operational vulnerabilities that could invalidate the bull or bear thesis.

## Integration with Google Search Grounding
- **Cite Verifiable Facts**: Always extract and present specific dates, source publication names, and exact quoted figures.
- **Fact vs Interpretation**: Distinctly separate raw empirical facts (e.g. "Q2 revenue was $24.5B, up 8% YoY") from market commentary or analytical interpretation.
- **No False Causation**: Never state a price movement was conclusively caused by a specific news headline unless credible reporting or econometric data directly substantiates the link.
