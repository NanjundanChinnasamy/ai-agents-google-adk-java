# Fundamental Analysis Skill

## Purpose & Scope
This skill provides a systematic multi-dimensional framework to evaluate a company's underlying financial health, operational viability, and competitive positioning. Fundamental analysis examines whether business performance supports current valuation.

## The Eight Analytical Pillars

### 1. Revenue & Earnings Growth
- **Top-Line Trajectory**: Is revenue growth organic (volume/pricing) or acquired (M&A)? Is it accelerating or decelerating year-on-year (YoY)?
- **Earnings Quality**: Is net income growing in tandem with revenue? Watch for one-off asset sales or tax benefits artificially inflating profits.

### 2. Profit Margins & Pricing Power
- **Gross Margin**: Reflects production efficiency and raw pricing power over inputs.
- **Operating Margin (EBIT Margin)**: Reflects operating efficiency and overhead control.
- **Net Margin**: Residual profitability after interest and taxes. Margin expansion indicates leverage; contraction signals cost pressures or pricing competition.

### 3. Return on Capital (Efficiency)
- **Return on Equity (ROE)**: $\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Shareholders' Equity}}$. Measures how effectively management uses equity capital.
- **Return on Capital Employed (ROCE)**: $\frac{\text{Operating Profit (EBIT)}}{\text{Capital Employed}}$. Evaluates returns against both equity and debt capital, providing a cleaner comparison across different capital structures.

### 4. Cash Flow Realities vs Accounting Profits
- Compare Net Income to **Operating Cash Flow**. If net income is consistently higher than cash flow from operations, investigate working capital bloat (growing accounts receivable or unsold inventory).

### 5. Balance Sheet Solvency & Debt Health
- **Leverage Ratios**: Debt-to-Equity (D/E), Net Debt / EBITDA.
- **Interest Coverage Ratio**: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest Expense}}$. Assesses safety cushion in servicing debt obligations during cyclical downturns.

### 6. Competitive Moat & Industry Position
- Network effects, intangible assets (patents, brands), switching costs, cost advantages, or scale efficiencies protecting market share from incumbents and new entrants.

### 7. Management Commentary & Execution Track Record
- Review quarterly earnings calls and annual filings for strategic consistency, capital allocation discipline, and adherence to past forward guidance.

### 8. Business & Sector Risks
- Cyclical vulnerabilities, input cost volatility, customer concentration, technological obsolescence, and regulatory scrutiny.

## Synthesis & Invariant
- **Holistic Evaluation**: Never assess an individual metric in isolation. A company with high revenue growth but negative free cash flow and heavy debt faces different solvency considerations than a mature company with modest growth and high ROE.
- **No Direct Advice**: Provide structured observations highlighting strengths, headwinds, and comparative trade-offs rather than buy or sell endorsements.
